Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

The IFRS16 Calculator website ("Service", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at www.ifrs16calculator.com.

By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Data You Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Email Address: When you submit your email to unlock the CSV export feature or subscribe to updates. This email is used to deliver the requested resource and for occasional communication about IFRS 16 updates, which you can opt-out of at any time.
  • Contact Form Information: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content to respond to your inquiry.

1.2 Calculator Data

Important: All calculations performed using our IFRS16 Calculator are processed locally in your web browser. Your lease payment amounts, interest rates, and other financial data entered into the calculator are never transmitted to our servers and are not stored or logged by us.

1.3 Automatically Collected Data

When you access the Service, we may automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, referring website
  • IP Address: Used for analytics and security purposes

This data is collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service
  • To deliver the CSV export feature you requested
  • To send periodic emails about IFRS 16 updates (with your consent)
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve our Service
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and hold certain information.

3.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the website (Google Analytics)
  • Advertising Cookies: Used to serve relevant advertisements (Google AdSense)

3.2 Google Services

We use the following Google services:

  • Google Analytics: To monitor and analyze website traffic and usage patterns. Google Analytics collects data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense: To display advertisements on our website. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3.3 Managing Cookies

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service.

The security of your data is important to us. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
