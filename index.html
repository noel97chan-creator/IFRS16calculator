Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IFRS 16 formula for Lease Liability?

The lease liability is calculated as the Present Value (PV) of future lease payments that are not paid at the commencement date, discounted using the interest rate implicit in the lease.

Is this calculator compliant with ASC 842?

Yes. While terminology differs slightly, the underlying math for calculating the present value of lease payments is identical for both IFRS 16 and ASC 842 standards.
