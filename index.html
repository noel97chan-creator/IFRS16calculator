Understanding IFRS 16 Calculation

This calculator determines the Present Value (PV) of future lease payments to establish the initial Lease Liability and Right-of-Use (ROU) Asset as required by International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS 16).

How to use this tool

  • Term: Enter the lease duration in months.
  • Rate (IBR): Enter your annual incremental borrowing rate.
