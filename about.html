About IFRS16 Calculator

IFRS16 Calculator is a free, professional-grade tool designed specifically for accountants, auditors, and finance professionals who work with lease accounting under international financial reporting standards.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make IFRS 16 lease accounting calculations accessible to everyone—from small accounting firms to individual auditors working on complex lease portfolios. We believe that accurate financial reporting tools shouldn't be locked behind expensive software subscriptions.

By providing a free, accurate, and easy-to-use calculator, we help finance professionals:

  • Save time on repetitive lease liability calculations
  • Reduce errors in amortization schedule generation
  • Generate audit-ready documentation with exportable CSV files
  • Better understand the impact of IFRS 16 on financial statements

Who We Serve

IFRS16 Calculator is designed primarily for finance professionals in Singapore, Malaysia, and the broader Asia-Pacific region, though our tool is used globally. Our typical users include:

Accountants

Preparing financial statements that comply with SFRS(I) 16, MFRS 16, or IFRS 16 requirements.

Auditors

Verifying lease calculations during statutory audits and ensuring compliance with reporting standards.

Finance Teams

Corporate finance professionals managing lease portfolios and preparing quarterly reports.

Students & Educators

Accounting students learning IFRS 16 concepts and lecturers demonstrating lease calculations.

Our Commitment to Quality

We take the accuracy of our calculations seriously. Our calculator is built on the standard present value annuity formulas prescribed by IFRS 16, and we regularly validate our outputs against professional accounting software.

Accurate Calculations

Our formulas follow IFRS 16/SFRS 16/MFRS 16 standards precisely, including proper handling of annuity due vs. ordinary annuity timing.

Privacy First

All calculations are performed locally in your browser. Your lease data never leaves your device or gets stored on our servers.

Always Available

Our tool is available 24/7 with no account registration required for basic calculations.

Regularly Updated

We monitor changes to accounting standards and update our tool and educational content accordingly.

Important Disclaimer

This tool is for educational and informational purposes only.

While we strive for accuracy, the IFRS16 Calculator does not constitute professional accounting, financial, or legal advice. The calculations are based solely on the inputs you provide and standard IFRS 16 formulae. The tool does not account for:

  • Complex lease modifications or reassessments
  • Variable lease payments or residual value guarantees
  • Foreign currency considerations
  • Local tax implications
  • Lease incentives or initial direct costs

You should always consult with a qualified accountant, CPA, or auditor before making financial decisions or preparing regulatory filings based on any calculations from this tool.
