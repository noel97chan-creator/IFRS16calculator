About IFRS16Pro

IFRS16Pro was developed to bridge the gap between complex accounting requirements and accessible technology. Since the implementation of IFRS 16 in 2019, many small to medium enterprises have struggled with the transition from operating leases to balance-sheet recognition.

Our Mission

To provide a transparent, audit-ready tool that helps accountants generate amortization schedules without the need for high-cost enterprise resource planning (ERP) software.

The Math Behind the Tool

Our calculator utilizes standard financial formulas for the Present Value of an Annuity. It accounts for both "Annuity Due" (payments at the start of the month) and "Ordinary Annuities" (payments in arrears).
